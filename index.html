Process

How the Compliance Engine Works

Every engagement follows the same four stages, so responsibilities stay clear at each hand-off.

Stage 1

Data Intake

The client supplies ledgers, bank statements, salary inputs and party masters through the agreed secure channel.

Stage 2

Rule Configuration

Payment-to-section mappings, rates and thresholds are recorded in a configuration sheet that the client, or the client's own professional advisor, approves.

Stage 3

Processing & Reconciliation

Computations run on the approved rules and are matched against challans and books. Items that fit no rule are listed in an exception report rather than decided by us.

Stage 4

Statement Preparation & Hand-off

Draft statements, registers, payslips and reports are returned with the exception list. Submission to any authority is made by the client or its authorised signatory.

Engagement Boundaries

  • Work is performed on information supplied by the client. Accuracy, completeness and the applicability of any provision remain the client's responsibility.
  • Esyton does not provide audit, attest, assurance, certification, tax advice, legal opinions or representation before any authority.
  • Statutory submissions are made by the client or its authorised signatory using the client's own credentials.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Esyton do?

Esyton Insights LLP provides back-office processing for accounting entries and reconciliations, payroll computation, and TDS/TCS workings and statement preparation. All work is done on records and instructions supplied by the client.

Who submits the TDS/TCS statements to the department?

Esyton prepares the statements in the prescribed format and returns them with a reconciliation and exception report. Submission is made by the client or its authorised signatory using the client's own credentials, unless a different arrangement is recorded in the engagement terms.

Does Esyton advise on whether TDS or TCS applies, or at what rate?

No. Esyton does not give tax advice. The engine applies section and rate mappings that the client, or the client's own professional advisor, has configured and approved. Where an item does not fit an approved mapping, it is listed in the exception report for the client to resolve.

Does Esyton audit, review or certify anything?

No. Esyton does not perform audit, attest, assurance or certification work and does not issue reports or opinions on financial statements. Management reports prepared from the books are for the client's internal use.

Is Esyton a firm of Chartered Accountants?

No. Esyton Insights LLP is a commercial entity and is not a firm of Chartered Accountants. It does not render services that are reserved by law for members of any professional body. Esyton does not hold itself out as providing any such service.

How is client data handled?

Client data is used only for the agreed processing. Access is limited to personnel assigned to the engagement, and files are held on encrypted cloud storage. Specific arrangements are recorded in the engagement terms.
