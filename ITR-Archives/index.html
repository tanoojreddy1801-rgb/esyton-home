1Regime snapshot

What survives in each regime

The new regime (Section 115BAC) is the default. Most Chapter VI-A deductions and several allowances are available only if you opt for the old regime.

Run your numbers both ways. A long list of old-regime claims only pays off if the total exceeds the new regime’s lower slab rates.
2Exemptions

Income that is exempt or partly exempt

Mostly Section 10 and Section 17 of the Income-tax Act, 1961, which governs AY 2025-26.
3Deductions

Chapter VI-A and salary deductions

Items under 80C, 80CCC and 80CCD(1) together share one ₹1.5 lakh limit. Do not claim the same payment twice.

4Donations

Section 80G: what qualifies

Donations are an old-regime-only deduction. The “qualifying limit” is 10% of your adjusted gross total income.

01

100%, no limit

For example, the Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund, National Defence Fund and PM CARES Fund.

02

50%, no limit

For example, the Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Fund and the Prime Minister’s Drought Relief Fund.

03

100%, within 10% limit

For example, Government or local authority donations for promoting family planning.

04

50%, within 10% limit

Most registered charitable trusts and NGOs, and Government for charitable purposes.

5Expenses

Property and other eligible expenses

Housing costs have the biggest swing between the two regimes.

6Capital gains

Reinvestment exemptions

Available under both regimes, but each has strict timelines. Stay within them.

7Avoid errors

Claims that go wrong most often

01

Wrong regime

Old-regime deductions claimed in the new regime are disallowed at processing.

02

Exceeding limits

80C, 80CCC and 80CCD(1) share one ₹1.5 lakh cap. Cap 80D and 80G amounts as well.

03

Cash payments

Cash donations above ₹2,000 and cash insurance premiums are not eligible.

04

Missing identifiers

Landlord PAN, donee PAN and ARN, and the lender’s certificate must match your claim.

05

Double claims

Do not claim HRA and 80GG together, or 80EE and 80EEA for the same loan.

06

Mismatch with Form 16

Compare your claims with Form 16, AIS and Form 26AS before you submit.

8A closing note

Thank you for reading this far. A claim is only as good as the proof behind it, so gather the documents before you file and keep them for future notices.
